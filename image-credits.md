# Image credits

Every photograph in `public/images/` comes from Pexels and is used under the Pexels licence, which permits
free commercial use without attribution. Credits are listed here anyway, because it is the right thing to do.

The Hosea Minds brand rules for imagery:

- East African people only, in calm, warm, reflective, or hopeful settings.
- No clinical imagery, no hospital corridors, no distress, no medical equipment.
- No stock portraits of named individuals presented as members. Testimonials use abstract initial avatars instead.
- No AI generated images, no watermarked previews.

## Not used, and why

Photographs showing hospital beds, clinical rooms, medical equipment, visibly distressed people, or grey
stock imagery were reviewed and deliberately rejected. A mental health platform should not make people feel
like patients before they have even signed in.

## Avatars

Testimonial avatars are CSS circles containing a single initial. They are not photographs, in keeping with
the promise that member stories are anonymised.

## Remote images

`next.config.ts` permits `images.pexels.com` so that any additional Pexels photography can be added later
without a config change. All images currently shipped live in `public/images/`.
